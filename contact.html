Membership

Join PUGS and become part of our growing community! Membership benefits include:

  • Priority access to garden plots
  • Discounted workshop fees
  • Members-only resource library
  • Voting rights in organizational decisions
  • Seed and plant discounts at our annual sale
  • Community and connection with fellow gardeners

Membership Fees

  • Individual: $25/year
  • Family: $40/year
  • Student/Senior: $15/year

How to Join: Email us at membership@pugs.org with your name, contact information, and membership type. We'll send you payment instructions and a welcome package.

Garden Plot Applications

Interested in your own garden plot? We accept applications year-round, though availability varies by location and season.

Application Process

  1. Email plots@pugs.org with your preferred garden location(s)
  2. Include a brief note about your gardening experience and goals
  3. We'll add you to our list and contact you when a plot becomes available
  4. Once assigned, you'll receive garden rules, guidelines, and an orientation

Plot Fees

  • Standard Plot (4x8): $50/season (includes PUGS membership)
  • Half Plot (4x4): $30/season (includes PUGS membership)

Note: All plot holders must be PUGS members. Plot fees include membership dues.

Volunteer Opportunities

PUGS relies on volunteers to maintain our gardens, organize events, and support our community. Ways to help:

  • Garden Maintenance: Help with weeding, mulching, and general upkeep
  • Workshop Leaders: Share your expertise with fellow gardeners
  • Event Support: Assist with our plant sales, tours, and festivals
  • Communications: Help with website, social media, or newsletter
  • Youth Programs: Support our children's gardening initiatives

Email volunteer@pugs.org to learn more about volunteer opportunities.

Donations

PUGS is a volunteer-run, non-profit organization. Your donations help us:

  • Maintain and expand our community gardens
  • Provide free or low-cost workshops
  • Support youth gardening programs
  • Purchase tools and supplies for shared use
  • Develop new garden sites

To make a donation, please contact us at info@pugs.org. We also gratefully accept donations of tools, materials, and plants.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need gardening experience to join?

Not at all! We welcome gardeners of all experience levels. Many of our members are beginners, and experienced gardeners are always happy to share advice.

Can I visit the gardens before applying for a plot?

Yes! Gardens are open daily dawn to dusk. Saturday mornings (10am-12pm) at Greenwood Garden are great times to visit, meet members, and ask questions.

Are the gardens organic?

Yes, all PUGS gardens follow organic practices. We do not allow synthetic pesticides or fertilizers.

What if I go on vacation?

We encourage garden buddies! Many members pair up to water and care for each other's plots during absences.

Is there a waitlist for plots?

Waitlist times vary by garden. Some gardens have plots available immediately, while popular locations may have a wait. Contact us for current availability.
