Regular Activities

Garden Work Parties

When: First Saturday of every month, 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Where: Rotating between all gardens

Monthly work parties keep our communal spaces beautiful and functional. All members are encouraged to attend at least one work party per season. Tasks include path maintenance, composting, tool care, and general garden upkeep.

Garden Hours

When: Daily, dawn to dusk

Where: All garden locations

Gardens are open daily for members to tend their plots. Remember to respect quiet hours (before 8am and after 8pm).

Informal Garden Meetups

When: Saturdays, 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Where: Greenwood Community Garden

Drop by on Saturday mornings for informal garden time. Great opportunity to meet other gardeners, ask questions, and share experiences.

Event Registration

To register for workshops and events, please contact us with the event name and date. We'll confirm your registration and send additional details.

Cancellation Policy: Please notify us at least 48 hours in advance if you cannot attend a registered event.

Host an Event

Have expertise to share? We welcome member-led workshops and activities. Contact us to propose an event or workshop.
