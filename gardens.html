Community Gardens

PUGS maintains several community garden sites across the peninsula. Each garden offers a unique environment for growing food, learning, and connecting with neighbors.

Our Gardens

Greenwood Community Garden

Location: Greenwood Park, Main Street

Size: 25 plots

Features: Full sun, water access, tool shed, composting area

Our largest and most established garden, Greenwood has been growing strong for over 10 years. The site features raised beds, a central gathering area, and excellent southern exposure.

Plot Availability: Waitlist (contact us to join)

Seaside Community Garden

Location: Ocean View Avenue

Size: 18 plots

Features: Partial shade, water access, tool storage, picnic area

Located near the waterfront, this garden benefits from moderate coastal climate. Perfect for leafy greens and cool-season crops. The site includes a small orchard with fruit trees.

Plot Availability: Limited plots available

Hillside Community Garden

Location: Hillside Road

Size: 20 plots

Features: Terraced beds, water access, greenhouse, rainwater collection

Built on a gentle slope with terraced beds to maximize space and prevent erosion. The greenhouse extends our growing season significantly.

Plot Availability: Plots available

Riverside Community Garden

Location: River Road

Size: 15 plots

Features: Rich soil, water access, accessible paths, children's garden area

Family-friendly garden with wide accessible paths and a dedicated children's garden area where young gardeners can learn and play.

Plot Availability: Plots available

Woodland Community Garden

Location: Forest Avenue

Size: 12 plots

Features: Partial shade, water access, native plant garden, wildlife habitat

This garden integrates food production with native plants and wildlife habitat. Ideal for shade-tolerant crops and pollinator-friendly plantings.

Plot Availability: Waitlist

Garden Plot Information

What's Included

  • Access to a 4' x 8' raised bed or equivalent ground plot
  • Water access for irrigation
  • Access to shared tools and equipment
  • Compost for soil amendments
  • Support and advice from experienced gardeners
  • Participation in garden events and workshops

Gardener Responsibilities

  • Maintain your plot using organic methods
  • Participate in communal garden maintenance days
  • Keep paths and common areas clear
  • Respect other gardeners and their plots
  • Follow the garden rules and guidelines

How to Apply

Interested in joining one of our community gardens? Visit our Contact page to submit an application. We accept applications year-round, though plot availability varies by season and location.

Garden Rules

  1. Use only organic gardening methods - no synthetic pesticides or fertilizers
  2. Respect plot boundaries and common areas
  3. Attend at least two garden work parties per season
  4. Keep plots actively maintained and productive
  5. Share knowledge and be welcoming to new gardeners
  6. Clean up after yourself and properly store tools
  7. Respect quiet hours (before 8am and after 8pm)
