About the Peninsula Urban Gardening Society

Our Mission

The Peninsula Urban Gardening Society (PUGS) is dedicated to promoting sustainable urban agriculture, fostering community connections, and improving food security in our region. We believe that everyone should have access to fresh, locally-grown food and the knowledge to grow their own.

Our History

Founded by a group of passionate gardeners and community advocates, PUGS has been serving the peninsula community for years. What started as a small group of neighbors sharing gardening tips has grown into a thriving organization that manages multiple community gardens and provides educational resources to hundreds of members.

Our journey began with the simple idea that urban spaces could be transformed into productive gardens. Through collaboration with local government, community organizations, and dedicated volunteers, we've created green spaces that nourish both body and spirit.

What We Believe

  • Sustainability: We promote organic and sustainable gardening practices that protect our environment
  • Community: Gardens are gathering places that bring diverse people together
  • Education: Knowledge sharing empowers everyone to grow their own food
  • Accessibility: Everyone deserves access to fresh, healthy food
  • Stewardship: We care for the land and pass it on better than we found it

Leadership

PUGS is led by a dedicated board of directors and supported by passionate volunteers. Our leadership team brings together expertise in horticulture, community development, education, and environmental sustainability.

We operate as a volunteer-run organization, with all members contributing their time and skills to maintain our gardens and support our community programs.

Get Involved

Whether you're an experienced gardener or just getting started, there's a place for you in PUGS. Join us to learn, grow, and connect with your community through the power of gardening.
